COSMOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy covers COSMOS for iPhone (App Store) and for Android (Google Play).

Summary

COSMOS is a free planetarium app. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and the developer does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data. All sky calculations run on your phone.

Location

COSMOS uses your location to work out which stars, planets and satellites are above you, to align the sky with true north and to calculate rise, set and pass times. These calculations happen entirely on your device.

If the cloud forecast is turned on, COSMOS sends a rounded location (about 10 km precision) to Open-Meteo to download the forecast. No identifier or other information is sent with it. You can turn the forecast off at any time in Settings.

Instead of using your current location, you can enter a place manually. When you search for a place by name, the search text is sent to a place-search service to find it: Apple's map service on iPhone, or the Open-Meteo geocoding service on Android. When you enter coordinates or paste a map link, COSMOS may ask the system geocoding service (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android) for the place name and time zone. If you paste a Google Maps short link, COSMOS contacts Google to expand it into coordinates. These requests are handled under Apple's, Open-Meteo's and Google's privacy policies.

Camera

The camera shows a live view of the sky behind the star overlay. The video is displayed only on your screen. It is never recorded, saved, analysed or transmitted.

Motion sensors

COSMOS reads the compass, gyroscope and accelerometer to know where your phone is pointing. This data is used only on your device, in real time.

Data downloaded by the app

COSMOS downloads public data that contains no personal information:

  • Satellite orbital elements from CelesTrak.
  • Hourly cloud forecasts from Open-Meteo (see Location above).
  • Updated star and deep-sky catalogues from the developer's public file host, if this feature is enabled.

As with any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address. COSMOS does not send them any other information about you.

Data stored on your device

Your favourites, observing log, display settings, chosen location and cached satellite and catalogue data are stored only on your phone. The developer never receives them. Deleting the app removes them. On Android, if you have turned on Google device backup, your settings, favourites and observing log may be included in that backup, which is stored in your own Google account and is not accessible to the developer.

Notifications

Reminders for sky events and satellite passes are scheduled locally on your phone. No notification data is sent to a server.

Children

COSMOS does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
