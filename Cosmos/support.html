Common questions

The sky overlay doesn't line up. Move your phone in a figure-of-eight to calibrate the compass, keep away from metal and magnets, and fine-tune with Settings → AR calibration.

I don't want to share my location. Turn on Settings → Use manual location and pick a place, type coordinates or paste a map link.

Satellites or the cloud forecast don't load. They need an internet connection once; satellite orbits are then cached for offline use.

Reminders don't appear. Allow notifications for COSMOS in your phone's settings.
