DECIBRA Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy covers DECIBRA for iPhone and DECIBRA for Android.

Summary

DECIBRA does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Microphone

DECIBRA uses the microphone only to measure sound in real time. Audio is analysed on your device and is never recorded, saved or transmitted.

Data stored on your device

When you record a session, DECIBRA saves measurement results such as sound levels, frequency spectra and exposure figures on your phone. Settings and the loud-event log are also stored on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export or share it yourself. On Android it is excluded from cloud backup and only moves if you transfer directly to a new phone. Deleting the app removes it.

Sound identification

Sound identification runs entirely on your device: on iPhone with Apple's SoundAnalysis framework, and on Android with Google's MediaPipe library and the bundled YAMNet model. No audio is sent to Apple, Google or anyone else. DECIBRA for Android does not even request internet access.

Widgets and Live Activities (iPhone)

Widgets and Live Activities display summaries that are shared only between DECIBRA and its widgets on your device.

Children

DECIBRA does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
