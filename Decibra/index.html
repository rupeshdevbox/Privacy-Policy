DECIBRA Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Summary

DECIBRA does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Microphone

DECIBRA uses the microphone only to measure sound in real time. Audio is analysed on your device and is never recorded, saved or transmitted.

Data stored on your device

When you record a session, DECIBRA saves measurement results such as sound levels, frequency spectra and exposure figures on your iPhone. Settings and the loud-event log are also stored on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export or share it yourself. Deleting the app removes it.

Sound identification

Sound identification uses Apple's on-device SoundAnalysis framework. No audio is sent to Apple or anyone else.

Widgets and Live Activities

Widgets and Live Activities display summaries that are shared only between DECIBRA and its widgets on your device.

Children

DECIBRA does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/rupeshdevbox/Privacy-Policy/issues.
