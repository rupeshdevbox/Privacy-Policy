Solar System Orrery Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy covers Solar System Orrery (shown on the Home Screen as "Solar Orrery") for iPhone and iPad.

Summary

Solar System Orrery is a free, offline 3D Solar System explorer for children and adults. It has no accounts, sign-in, analytics, advertising, in-app purchases, third-party SDKs or tracking. The app does not connect to the internet, and the developer does not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal data. Everything the app calculates, generates and remembers stays on your device.

No internet connection

All planet positions, moons, sky events, quiz questions, textures, sounds and narration are computed or generated on the device. The app makes no network requests, so no information about you or your device is sent anywhere by the app.

Camera

The camera is used only for the optional "In Your Room" augmented-reality view, which places a model of the Solar System on a table or floor. The camera image is shown only on your screen and is processed on the device by Apple's ARKit to detect surfaces. It is never recorded, saved, analysed for faces or people, or transmitted. You can decline camera access and use the rest of the app normally, and change this later in the Settings app.

Information you enter

The "Me in Space" game lets you enter a birthday and a weight to show your age and weight on other worlds. These values are stored only on your device, are used only to show those results, and are never sent anywhere. You can change them at any time, and deleting the app removes them.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • Display and sound settings, and whether the introduction has been shown.
  • Your best Space Quiz score and the worlds you have stamped in the Space Passport.
  • The birthday, weight and unit entered in "Me in Space".

The developer never receives this data. Deleting the app removes it. If you use iCloud or computer backups, it may be included in your own device backup under Apple's terms; the developer cannot access it.

Sound and narration

The ambient soundscape is generated by the app, and guided-tour narration uses the speech voices built into your device. The app does not use the microphone and does not record audio.

Children

Solar System Orrery is designed to be safe for children. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, contains no advertising or links that leave the app, and has no chat, sharing or social features. Parents and guardians can contact the developer with any questions using the details below.

Changes
