Solar System Orrery Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy covers Solar System Orrery (shown on the Home Screen as "Solar Orrery") for iPhone and iPad (App Store) and for Android (Google Play). The app is published by Rupesh Pandey ("the developer").

Summary

Solar System Orrery is a free, offline 3D Solar System explorer for children, students and adults. It has no accounts, sign-in, analytics, advertising, in-app purchases, third-party SDKs or tracking. The app does not connect to the internet, and the developer does not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal data. Everything the app calculates, generates and remembers stays on your device.

Data collected and shared

  • Data collected: none. No personal or device data is collected by the developer.
  • Data shared with third parties: none.
  • Data sold: none.

No internet connection

All planet positions, moons, sky events, quiz questions, textures, sounds and narration are computed or generated on the device. The app makes no network requests (on Android it does not request the Internet permission), so no information about you or your device is sent anywhere by the app.

Camera

The camera is used only for the optional "In Your Room" augmented-reality view, which places a model of the Solar System on a table or floor. The camera image is shown only on your screen and is processed on the device to detect surfaces: by Apple's ARKit on iPhone and iPad, and by Google Play Services for AR (ARCore) on Android. The image is never recorded, saved, analysed for faces or people, or transmitted by the app. You can decline camera access and use the rest of the app normally, and change this later in your device settings.

On Android, this application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google LLC and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Information you enter

The "Me in Space" game lets you enter a birthday and a weight to show your age and weight on other worlds. These values are stored only on your device, are used only to show those results, and are never sent anywhere. You can change them at any time, and deleting the app removes them.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • Display and sound settings, and whether the introduction has been shown.
  • Your best Space Quiz score and the worlds you have stamped in the Space Passport.
  • The birthday, weight and unit entered in "Me in Space".

The developer never receives this data. Deleting the app removes it. If you use iCloud or computer backups, it may be included in your own device backup under Apple's terms; on Android, if Google device backup is turned on, it may be included in the backup stored in your own Google account. The developer cannot access either.

Deleting your data

Because nothing is stored outside your device, you can delete all of the app's data at any time by uninstalling the app, or on Android by opening Settings → Apps → Solar Orrery → Storage → Clear storage. There is no account to delete.

Sound and narration

The ambient soundscape is generated by the app, and guided-tour narration uses the speech voices built into your device. The app does not use the microphone and does not record audio.

Children

Solar System Orrery is designed to be safe for children and students. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, contains no advertising or links that leave the app, and has no chat, sharing or social features. The app is intended to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Apple's App Store Kids Category guidelines and the Google Play Families Policy. Parents and guardians can contact the developer with any questions using the details below.

Security

Because the app does not transmit or store any data off your device, there is no server-side data that could be exposed. Data on your device is protected by your device's own security, such as its passcode and encryption.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
