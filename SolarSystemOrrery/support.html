Common questions

How do I move around? Drag with one finger to orbit, pinch to zoom and drag with two fingers to pan. Tap any world, or use the planet bar, to fly to it. Double-tap empty space to return to the overview.

"In Your Room" doesn't find a surface. Allow camera access, make sure the room is well lit, and move your iPhone slowly over a table or the floor. This view needs a device that supports ARKit.

I can't hear the soundscape or narration. Check that the Silent switch is off and the volume is up, and that sound is turned on with the speaker button.

How accurate are the planet positions? Planets follow JPL orbital elements valid from 1800 to 2050. Distances and sizes are scaled so every world fits on screen.

How do I reset the Space Passport or quiz score? Delete and reinstall the app. All progress is stored only on your device.
